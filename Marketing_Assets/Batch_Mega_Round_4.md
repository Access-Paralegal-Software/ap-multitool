# Blog 29: Texas Rule 11 Agreements: The "Secret Weapon" of Settlement
**Date:** 2026-11-23
---
In Texas litigation, a "Rule 11 Agreement" is one of the most powerful tools in your arsenal. It is the only way to ensure that an agreement between counsel is legally binding and enforceable by the court. 

Under TRCP 11, for an agreement to be enforceable, it must be (1) in writing, (2) signed, and (3) filed with the papers as part of the record, or made in open court on the record.

We help you draft clear, precise Rule 11 agreements for everything from deposition scheduling to final settlement terms. Don't rely on a handshake or a vague email. Get it in writing, get it filed, and get it enforced.

# Blog 32: Remote Depositions in Texas: 2026 Best Practices
**Date:** 2026-12-14
---
Remote depositions are here to stay. But the "Zoom-from-the-Kitchen" era is over. Professionalism and security are the new standards.

Under TRCP 199, a remote deposition must be recorded and conducted with specific safeguards. 
- **The Record:** Who is responsible for the digital original?
- **The Exhibits:** How are they being shared and "marked" in real-time?
- **The Environment:** Reducing distractions and ensuring a court-ready background.

We coordinate the technical side of your remote depositions, ensuring a seamless, high-fidelity record every time.
