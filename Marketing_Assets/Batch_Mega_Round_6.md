# Blog 41: Texas Subpoena Reach: The 150-Mile Rule in 2026
**Date:** 2027-02-15
---
A Texas subpoena is powerful, but it has geographic limits. Under TRCP 176.3, you can't require a person to appear more than 150 miles from where they reside or were served. 

We help you navigate these limits, coordinating remote depositions or local document productions that keep your evidence gathering on track without violating the rules. 

Know your reach. Perfect your service.
