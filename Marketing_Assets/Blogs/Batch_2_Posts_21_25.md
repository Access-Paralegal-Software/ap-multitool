**Date:** 2026-10-05
---

Our **"Production Lock" Protocol** ensures zero-error delivery.

# Blog 23: The Texas "Apex" Doctrine: Protecting Your Client’s Executives
**Date:** 2026-10-12
---
In Texas law, the "Apex Doctrine" is a vital shield for high-ranking corporate executives. It prevents opposing counsel from deposing your client’s CEO or President simply to harass the company, unless they can prove the executive has "unique or superior personal knowledge" of the facts.

If you’re defending a corporate client, you need to know the requirements of the *Crown Central Petroleum* test. 
- Has the executive been shown to have unique knowledge?
- Have you exhausted less-intrusive discovery methods (like depositions of lower-level managers)?

We help you gather the evidence and draft the motions needed to invoke the Apex Doctrine and keep your executives where they belong: running their company, not sitting in a deposition chair.
