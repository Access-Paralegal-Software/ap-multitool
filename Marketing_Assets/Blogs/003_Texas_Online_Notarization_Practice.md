# What Texas Online Notarization Actually Looks Like in Practice

The days of the "Notary Hunt" are over. You know the drill: a client needs a document notarized for a filing tomorrow morning, but they’re in a meeting in Dallas, you’re in a deposition in Houston, and your office notary just stepped out for lunch.

Since Texas legalized Remote Online Notarization (RON), that entire logistical nightmare has been condensed into a 10-minute video call. But I still hear from many attorneys who are hesitant. *Is it secure? Is it accepted by the court? How does it actually work?*

As an active Texas Online Notary, here is what the process actually looks like from the inside.

### 1. The Technology Foundation
RON is not just "notarizing over Zoom." Under Texas law, we must use a state-approved platform that provides secure audio-video communication, credential analysis, and identity proofing. The platform records the entire session and creates an encrypted "digital tamper-evident seal" on the document.

### 2. Identity Proofing (KBA)
Before the session starts, the signer must pass **Knowledge-Based Authentication (KBA)**. This is the "What car did you own in 2012?" style quiz. It’s significantly more secure than simply glancing at a physical ID, which can be forged. The platform also performs a high-res scan and analysis of their government-issued ID to ensure its authenticity.

### 3. The Digital Signing
During the live video session, the signer and I both apply digital signatures. As the notary, I apply my digital seal. The moment that seal is placed, the document is "locked." If even a single comma is changed after the fact, the digital certificate will show that the document has been tampered with.

### 4. The Digital Record
One of the biggest advantages for Texas law firms is the record-keeping. The notary is required to maintain a secure video recording of the session and a digital journal for at least 10 years. If the validity of a signature is ever challenged in court, you don’t have to rely on a notary’s memory—you have the video evidence.

### Why Your Clients Love It
For your clients, the value is **convenience**. They can sign from their kitchen table or their hotel room. No driving across town, no printing, and no "wet ink" mailing delays. 

### Why You Should Love It
For the attorney, RON means **speed and defensibility**. You get your executed documents back in minutes, not days, and you have a bulletproof audit trail for every signature.
