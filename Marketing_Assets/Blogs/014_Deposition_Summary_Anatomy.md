# The Anatomy of a Great Deposition Summary

You’ve just finished an eight-hour deposition of the defendant’s lead engineer. You know they made some critical admissions, but they’re buried somewhere in 350 pages of transcript. 

When the transcript arrives from the court reporter, do you have the time to read it cover-to-cover again? Do you want to pay an associate to do it? 

A great deposition summary is more than just a "shorter version" of the transcript. It is a **Strategic Map** of the testimony. At Access Paralegal Services, we’ve summarized thousands of hours of testimony. here is what a winning summary looks like.

### 1. The Three Columns
We use a standardized three-column format:
- **Page/Line Reference:** Exactly where the testimony lives.
- **Subject:** A brief heading (e.g., "Training History" or "Knowledge of the Defect").
- **Summary:** A concise, present-tense distillation of the testimony.

### 2. The "Key Admissions" Section
Every summary should start with a one-page "Executive Summary" of the most critical admissions. If you only have five minutes to prep for a hearing, this page tells you exactly what you need to know. 

### 3. The "Impeachment" Hook
A great summary doesn't just record what was said; it highlights the **inconsistencies**. 
*"At P. 142, Witness claims they never saw the email. This contradicts Exhibit 4 (Production Set B) which shows they replied to the thread."* 
These hooks are the ammunition you need for trial cross-examination.

### 4. Topic-Based vs. Chronological
- **Chronological:** Best for understanding the flow of the deposition as it happened.
- **Topic-Based:** Best for preparing for a Motion for Summary Judgment or Trial. 
We often provide both, allowing you to use the testimony in different ways as the case evolves.

### 5. Why Precision Matters
A deposition summary is only as good as its page references. If the summary says "P. 14" but the testimony is on "P. 41," the summary is worse than useless—it’s a distraction. We perform a final audit on every summary to ensure every reference is 100% accurate.
