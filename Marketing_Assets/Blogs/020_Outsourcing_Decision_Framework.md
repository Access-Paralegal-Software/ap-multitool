# When to Outsource: A Decision Framework for Solo Attorneys

You’re busy. You’re stressed. You’re successful—but you’re tired. 

You know you need help, but the thought of hiring a full-time employee feels overwhelming. On the other hand, you’re worried that outsourcing to a freelancer might be "one more thing to manage." 

How do you know when you’ve reached the tipping point? At Access Paralegal Services, we’ve helped dozens of attorneys make this transition. Here is the **Decision Framework** we use to help you decide if it’s time to outsource.

### 1. The "Hourly Rate" Test
What is your hourly billable rate? Let’s say it’s $350. 
Now, look at your task list. Are you spending time on:
- Bates stamping a production?
- Summarizing a deposition?
- Formatting a Table of Authorities?
- Coordinating a process server?
If you are spending even 5 hours a week on these tasks, you are essentially "paying" yourself **$1,750 a week** to do paralegal work. That is $91,000 a year in lost revenue. If you can outsource those 5 hours for a fraction of that cost, the math is simple. 

### 2. The "Bottleneck" Test
Look at your current case queue. Is a motion sitting on your desk because the cite-checking isn't done? Is a production delayed because the privilege log is a mess? 
If your **growth** is limited by your **capacity** to handle the "Heavy Lifting" of litigation, you have a bottleneck. Outsourcing allows you to expand your capacity instantly without the long-term risk of a new hire.

### 3. The "Expertise" Test
Do you have a case that involves 5,000 emails and an ESI protocol? Do you have a complex merger that requires dozens of SOS filings? 
If a case requires specialized technical skills that you don't use every day, it is more efficient to outsource to a specialist than to spend 20 hours "learning" it on the fly. 

### 4. The "Lifestyle" Test
Why did you start your own firm? Most solos say "Freedom" or "Autonomy." 
If you are working 70 hours a week and haven't taken a vacation in two years, you don't have freedom—you have a high-stress job with an unforgiving boss (yourself). Outsourcing is the key to reclaiming your life. 

### The Decision Matrix
| Factor | Hire Full-Time? | Outsource Freelance? |
|---|---|---|
| **Volume** | Consistent 40+ hrs/wk | Fluctuating or "Burst" needs |
| **Budget** | High ($70k+ total cost) | Low (Pay-per-case/hour) |
| **Risk** | High (Employment liability) | Low (Contractual partnership) |
| **Expertise** | Generalist | Specialist |
