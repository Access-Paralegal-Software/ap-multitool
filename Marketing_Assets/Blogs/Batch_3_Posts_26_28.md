# Blog 26: Navigating Harris County E-Filing: Tips from the Trenches
**Date:** 2026-11-02
---
If you’re a Texas litigator, you know the specific "heart-sink" of receiving an e-filing rejection from the Harris County District Clerk at 4:55 PM. 

Most rejections in Harris County aren't because of the law; they’re because of the formatting. 
- **The "Lead Document" Error:** Are you attaching your exhibits as a single PDF or as separate attachments?
- **The Searchable PDF Rule:** Is your filing truly OCR'd, or just a scan?
- **The Redaction Trap:** Are you using a "Black Box" that can be removed, or a true digital redaction?

We’ve filed thousands of documents in Harris County. We know the local quirks, and we ensure your filing is "Accepted" the first time, every time.

# Blog 27: Automated Exhibit Numbering: From Review to Trial
**Date:** 2026-11-09
---
During the review phase of a case, you might have "Hot Document #42." By the time you get to trial, that document is "Plaintiff’s Exhibit 114." 

If your team is manually tracking these changes in a spreadsheet, you’re asking for trouble. 

The **Access Paralegal Multitool** creates a "Digital Thread" for every document. As you move from review to production to trial, the tool tracks the metadata, the Bates number, and the Exhibit number in one unified database. 

When the judge asks for the document, you aren't searching through three different lists. You’re clicking one button.
