# Blog 47: Why "Self-Collection" is a Malpractice Risk
**Date:** 2027-03-29
---
If you tell your client to "just send me the relevant emails," you are inviting a spoliation claim. 

Clients don't understand metadata. They don't understand MIME structures. They forward emails, which changes the headers, or they "copy and paste" text, which destroys the integrity of the evidence. 

Professional eDiscovery starts with professional collection. We pull the data in its native format, ensuring that every metadata byte is preserved and every production is defensible.

# Blog 48: Texas Rule 202: Discovery Before Suit
**Date:** 2027-04-05
---
In Texas, you don't always have to file a lawsuit to get discovery. **Rule 202** allows you to petition the court for a deposition or document production to "investigate a potential claim." 

This is a powerful, underutilized tool for Texas litigators. 

We help you coordinate Rule 202 discovery, providing the technical support needed to pull evidence *before* you commit to a full lawsuit. Find out if the case is worth it before you spend the client's money.
