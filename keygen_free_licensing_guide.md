# 🔐 Keygen.sh: How to Issue Free Tester License Keys

This guide outlines the exact steps to generate and issue **100% free, lifetime evaluation keys** to friends, family, and beta testers for the Access Paralegal Multitool. 

*These steps bypass payment gateways completely and cost you nothing.*

---

### 🎯 Step 1: Access the Licensing Center
1. Log into the Keygen dashboard at: [dashboard.keygen.sh](https://dashboard.keygen.sh/)
2. In the left sidebar, click on **Licenses**.

---

### ⚙️ Step 2: Generate the License
1. Click the blue **+ Create License** button in the top-right corner.
2. Fill in the following fields (leave everything else blank/default):
   * **Product**: Select `Access Paralegal Multitool` from the dropdown.
   * **Policy**: Select `Lifetime Unlimited License` from the dropdown.
   * **Name** (Optional): Type the recipient's name (e.g., *"John Doe - Beta Tester"*). 
   * **Status**: Ensure this is set to `Active`.

---

### 💾 Step 3: Save & Copy the Key
1. Scroll to the bottom and click **Create License**.
2. The screen will refresh, revealing the license details.
3. Look for the **Key** field (it looks like a unique alphanumeric string, e.g., `XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).
4. Copy this string and send it to your tester!

---

### 💡 Tips for Managing Testers
* **Preventing Over-Sharing**: Each key generated using this policy is limited to **1 machine**. If a tester tries to share their key with 10 people, it will automatically block the additional computers!
* **Revoking Access**: If you ever need to cancel a free key, just find it in the **Licenses** list, click on it, and click **Suspend** or **Delete**. It will instantly lock the app on their computer!
